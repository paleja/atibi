# I2S DAC: IQaudIO Pi-DAC+ (MPN SC2078), and any other PCM5122/PCM5121 based
# I2S DAC. The stock analog output has to stay off: it holds the I2S clock
# domain, and the Pi-DAC+ mutes its RCA jacks while the headphone amp is live.
dtparam=i2c_arm=on
dtoverlay=iqaudio-dacplus
dtoverlay=vc4-kms-v3d,noaudio
